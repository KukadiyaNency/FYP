# Sectoral impact analysis of covid-19 pandemic in the UK. 
**Analysing the long-term effects of COVID-19 on various industries using ONS data.**  

## Project Overview  
This project investigates how the COVID-19 pandemic influenced the UK business environment across five key sectors:
* Accommodation and Food Service Activities (Hospitality)
* Wholesale and Retail Trade (Retail)
* Manufacturing
* Transportation and Storage
* Information and Communication
  
## Project Objectives
1. Examine financial evolution across key sectors
2. Analyse workforce and employment trends (e.g., furlough)
3. Study trade resilience and disruption in exports/imports
4. Identify strategic responses (e.g., government schemes uptake)

- It explores sectoral impacts through:
📊 Financial Performance
👷 Workforce Dynamics
🌍 Trade (Import & Export) Variations
🛠️ Strategic Business Adaptations

## Insights & Learnings
- Pandemic impacts were sector-specific, with hospitality being the most vulnerable
- Workforce furlough patterns mirrored business activity slowdowns
- Strategic government support uptake varied widely
- Trade challenges fluctuated significantly based on global supply chain dynamics
